Privacy

This page covers this training site only — not the Minute app, and not awign.com. A final version will be published after legal review.

What this site does

It teaches people in Japan how to record paid first-person video for the AI and robotics data programme of Awign (Mynavi Group), and gives them the organization code to start recording in the Minute app.

What we collect here

  • Nothing personal is sent from this site. There is no sign-up form. The LINE buttons only open our LINE Official Account.

LINE and payments

LINE's own privacy policy applies on our LINE account and OpenChat. Details you give us for payment (name, Minute email, payout account) are used only to verify your work and pay you, and are never sold. Payout details for Japan are coming soon.

The Minute app

Once you install Minute, that app's privacy policy and consent screens govern your recordings and account data. This site is separate from it.

Recordings and other people

Never record faces, ID documents, bank cards, addressed mail, nameplates, screens or personal papers. Only record where everyone present knows and agrees, and only during the task.

We never ask for money

No fee, charge or deposit at any stage. If someone asks you to pay in Awign's name, don't — tell us on LINE.
